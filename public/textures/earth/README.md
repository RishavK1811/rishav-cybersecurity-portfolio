# Earth Texture Asset Sources & Attribution

The Earth textures used in this 3D visualization originate from high-resolution imagery released by NASA and USGS into the public domain, packaged via the open-source Three.js examples repository.

### 1. Earth Day Surface Texture (`earth_day_2048.jpg`)
- **Source**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Visible Earth ("The Blue Marble").
- **Repository**: Three.js Examples (`examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg`).
- **License**: Public Domain (NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted and may be used for educational, scholarly, and commercial purposes in accordance with NASA guidelines).
- **Resolution**: 2048 x 1024 equirectangular map featuring deep-blue oceans, natural green/brown continental landmasses, and polar ice caps.

### 2. Earth Clouds Texture (`earth_clouds_1024.png`)
- **Source**: NASA Earth Observatory cloud coverage composites.
- **Repository**: Three.js Examples (`examples/textures/planets/earth_clouds_1024.png`).
- **License**: Public Domain (NASA Earth Observatory).
- **Resolution**: 1024 x 512 grayscale coverage mask utilized as an opacity/alpha map for natural cloud formations.

### 3. Earth Specular Map (`earth_specular_2048.jpg`)
- **Source**: NASA Visible Earth topography and water body mask.
- **Repository**: Three.js Examples (`examples/textures/planets/earth_specular_2048.jpg`).
- **License**: Public Domain (NASA).
- **Resolution**: 2048 x 1024 binary specular mask where water surfaces reflect specular highlights while landmasses remain matte.
